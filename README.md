# ⚖️ LEX AI

### Indian Legal Information Assistant

LEX AI is an intelligent legal workspace designed to help organize cases, documents, and legal research in one place.

## 🚀 Live Demo

### 🌐 [Open LEX AI](nova-ai-silk-zeta.vercel.app)

## ✨ Features

- 📁 Case management
- 📄 Legal document management
- 🤖 LEX AI legal assistant
- 💬 AI conversations
- 🔐 Secure authentication
- ☁️ Cloud-based data storage
- 🔎 Workspace search
- 👤 Account settings

## 🧠 LEX AI

LEX is designed to assist with:

- Legal information
- Document summarization
- Case analysis
- Legal research
- Understanding legal concepts

> **Disclaimer:** LEX provides general legal information and research assistance. It does not replace professional legal advice.
